Our Story

Authentic Nigerian Flavours, Right Here in The Pearl

BEKES was born to bring the taste of home to Doha — home-made African cooking with the finest ingredients, in a setting that feels like family.

Whether you're a fellow Nigerian craving that familiar jollof smoke, or curious about West African cuisine for the first time, our team will walk you through every dish with a smile.

  • Home-made, authentic recipes
  • Live music & terrace seating
  • Generous, family-style portions
  • Dine-in, takeaway & delivery
