The Story of BEKES

A taste of home in the heart of The Pearl — where culture, flavour and hospitality meet, one plate at a time.

Who We Are

A Cultural Experience, Not Just a Meal

BEKES is a home for bold West African flavour in Doha — a women-owned restaurant that brings authentic Nigerian cuisine right into the heart of The Pearl.

Walk in and you'll immediately feel it: a warm, lively space rooted in African heritage, with marble tables, velvet chairs, a purple neon heartbeat, and music that keeps the evening moving.

For Nigerians in Qatar, we're a familiar taste of home — a reminder of the flavours and traditions you grew up with. For everyone else, we're a friendly gateway into dishes that are comforting and exciting at once. The aromas that greet you at the door say the rest.

Everything we serve is home-made and halal — from smoky party jollof to slow-simmered soups, fresh grilled suya and the sweet, fluffy puff puff that arrives before your meal.

What began as one much-loved dining room has grown into two locations in Doha — the original BEKES Pearl and Bekes Express on Museum Street — so even more of the city can taste real jollof and genuine Naija hospitality.

What We Stand For

Three Things We Never Compromise On

Authentic Recipes

Home-made Nigerian and West African cooking — jollof done properly with smoke, soups simmered with patience, and spice blends that respect the tradition.

The Finest Ingredients

Fresh meat, fish and produce, prepared daily by grilling, simmering and stewing. Dishes are made to order so your meal arrives at its peak.

Warm Hospitality

From the complimentary puff puff on arrival to patient explanations of every dish, our team treats you like family — and the live music makes it feel that way.

The Space

Designed to Make You Stay a While

A calm, stylish dining room in the middle of The Pearl's buzz — elegant marble tables with gold detailing, plush seating, floral columns and a terrace with views. People typically spend 45 minutes to 4 hours here, and we think that says enough.
